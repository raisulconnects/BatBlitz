About BatBlitz

BatBlitz is a turn-based cricket game where you play quick matches against the computer. Choose your mode, win the toss, and decide whether to bat or bowl first.

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no runtime — just pure fun.
